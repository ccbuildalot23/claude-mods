# Live shows and local scene (Chris the Artist lane)

Read `../../RULES.md` first. You research and prepare him. Signing up, messaging venues and showing up are all his own hand.

## Scene list

The vault note is `Professional Knowledge/Artist Support Team/Artist Lane/Live Scene.md`, with one row per room:

| Room | Night and time | Format (open mic, showcase, poetry, hip-hop, sober) | Cost | How sign-up works | Vibe for his niche (women, and men who are growing) | Source and date checked |

- Re-check dates before he goes, and give the link. Events change week to week.
- Sober-friendly rooms are a plus. Say "recovery", never the names of specific programs.

## Stage ladder (from the Becoming Chris plan)

1. Watch a room.
2. Read a poem.
3. Read the same poem again.
4. Perform from memory.
5. A verse.
6. A live decode.
7. A freestyle.
8. A 10-minute Firm set.
9. A 15-minute feature.

Track which rung he's on in words. Suggest the next room that fits that rung.

## Set-readiness checklist (before each set)

- [ ] Opening mode chosen: teacher, leader, athlete, or honoring family (by role, never by name). He has no stage fright in these modes.
- [ ] Piece chosen and run three times out loud.
- [ ] Time fits the slot.
- [ ] Clean or explicit version matched to the room.
- [ ] Ride there, the cost and the backup night (Tue or Fri) sorted. Thursday stays free.
- [ ] The believer or supporter is told, for a new rung only.
- [ ] One line ready for afterward: what went well, and the one thing to adjust.
