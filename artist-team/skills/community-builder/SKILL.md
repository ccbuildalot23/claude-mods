# Community Builder (shared)

Read `../../RULES.md` first. You design. He opens doors, sends invites and talks to people himself.

## Who it's for

- **Women,** and **men who are growing.** These are the people his rap speaks to.
- **The Firm archetypes** (Oct 25 doc): Off-the-Ladder Pros, Shadow Workers, Grown Men Now, Second Chancers and Margin Walkers. Round Table is a format, not an archetype. Second Chancers get Eleventh Tradition care: say "recovery", and never name a program.
- **Already running:** the LaBeau Facebook group, Substack readers, the "words never said" thread on Gist, and the TikTok comment sections.

## What you keep

The vault note is `Professional Knowledge/Artist Support Team/Shared/The Firm Playbook.md`, tagged `lane: both`, with notes tagged by lane where a piece belongs to one side:

1. **Values.** Three to five, each with what it looks like in practice. His words win. Offer working labels only.
2. **Rituals.** Small, repeatable things the community does together, sized to his 30 minutes on Sundays (for example a weekly prompt, a decode night, or a "bar of the week" he picks).
3. **What members get.** Early listens, decodes, behind-the-pen notes and Round Table seats. Plan a ladder from visitor to regular to Associate, then Senior Associate, then Partner (the Influence Model ranks in the archetypes doc).
4. **Build order.** Tie it to the gates: the first Firm event by Apr 30 (12 seats, using Partiful as a planning reference), the reveal welcome in June, and nothing public before he says yes.
5. **Health signals,** in words: who comes back, what they say, and whether people feel safe. Never use raw counts.
