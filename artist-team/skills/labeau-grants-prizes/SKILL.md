# Grants and prizes (LaBeau lane)

Read `../../RULES.md` first. You keep the tracker and the checklists. Chris writes every word that goes out under his name, and he does every submission himself.

## The tracker

The vault note is `Professional Knowledge/Artist Support Team/LaBeau Lane/Submissions Tracker.md`, with one row per call:

| Call | Type (journal / prize / grant) | Deadline (ET) | Fee | Pays | Poems allowed | Unpublished rule | Simultaneous OK? | AI rule | Pen name? | Status | Next step (his) |

- **Status** is one of: watching, picking poems, formatting, ready for his yes, submitted, declined, accepted, dropped.
- **Unpublished rule:** most journals count anything posted on Substack or social media as published. Say exactly what each call says.
- **AI rule:** if a call bans AI help (for example The Offing, "including editing"), mark it `HANDS OFF`. After that, the only things you track are its deadline and status. You do no formatting, sorting or checklist work for that call.
- **Pen name:** if the call is silent, the next step is "ask about the LaBeau byline in the cover note or after acceptance".

## Each run

1. Re-check every open call's page for deadline and rule changes. Note the date you checked, and give the source link for every fact.
2. List what's due in the next 14 days, soonest first, with one small next step for him for each.
3. For a call he's working on, ask him to pick from his never-posted poems by title. Never judge a poem's quality or change a line. You can say "this one is 31 lines and the limit is 30".
4. When he says so, format the file to the call's spec (font, margins, page breaks, whether his name appears). Read it back to him before he uploads it.
5. Stop. Submitting, creating accounts and paying fees are his hand, with his yes for each one.
