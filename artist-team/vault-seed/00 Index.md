# Artist Support Team

Chris's team, run as skills from the claude-mods `artist-team` plugin. Every role gives advice; Chris decides and does every outward step himself. Rules: `~/Developer/claude-mods/artist-team/RULES.md`.

| Role | Skill | Lane | Its notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Artist Manager | artist-manager | artist | Artist Lane/Song Pipeline, Artist Lane/Status YYYY-MM |
| Craft & A&R Coach | craft-ar-coach | artist, labeau | Artist Lane/A&R - ..., LaBeau Lane/Craft - ... |
| Live shows and local scene | artist-live-scene | artist | Artist Lane/Live Scene |
| Grants and prizes | labeau-grants-prizes | labeau | LaBeau Lane/Submissions Tracker |
| Reveal path | labeau-reveal-path | labeau | LaBeau Lane/Reveal Path |
| Brand Builder | brand-builder | both | Shared/Brand Story |
| Community Builder | community-builder | both | Shared/The Firm Playbook |
| Knowledge Harvester | knowledge-harvester | both | method cards (Creative Methods Library; public cards in grok-bridge/research/artist-knowledge/) |
| Audience Researcher | labeau-research subagent | both | research notes, tagged by lane |

Ask in any Claude Code session, for example: "A&R notes on my newest freestyle", "what's due for submissions", or "monthly status".
