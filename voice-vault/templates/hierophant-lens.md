# The Hierophant Lens

Every agent reads this before planning anything for LaBeau or for Chris: a post, caption, hook, Substack Note, email, Reel, Gist post, research brief or social plan. Grok, Muse, the labeau-ops builder, ChatGPT, Perplexity and any Claude thread.

LaBeau and Chris's personal brand are one brand now. The diary is the work.

## Chris, in his own words (Oct 23, 2026)

> I'm a hierophant and my purpose is to share my perspective with a sacred bunch. I want to make sure they get how they're supposed to and how I like for them to see it as well - and that it represents me, authentically, fully, and like Rick Rubin's creative act - my diary

> I especially want it considered as we continue thinking of how to make sure my brand stands out and we package me and my diary and who I am so that my story finds its audience naturally and invites them to my experience and how we view the world.

## What that means when you plan (agent guidance, not Chris's words)

1. **The diary is the art.** Present his lines as pages from a living diary, not as "content". The page, the pen and the hand belong in the frame.
2. **Show, don't preach.** A hierophant reveals what is sacred and lets people in. No advice voice, no "you should", no guru framing. Invite the reader into his experience; never lecture them.
3. **Write to the sacred bunch, not to everyone.** Aim for the few who will feel at home. A truer post for fewer people beats a louder post for more.
4. **Whole and authentic.** Across a week, the reader should meet all of him that is public: poet, voice and rapper, the one who studies how people grow. One post can be one facet; the plan carries the whole.
5. **Process is part of the work** (Rubin, The Creative Act). Craft, attention, quiet, the moment a line arrived: these are worth showing.
6. **Let it find its audience naturally.** No tactic that costs authenticity: no bait, no fake urgency, no trend that is not his.
7. **Stop the scroll, draw in, feel at home.** Use his color research on every visual.

## Hard rules (always)

- Chris's words are never changed. Poems, diary lines and share-candidates are quoted exactly. Captions, hooks and Notes go around his words, never inside them.
- Agents write the full caption (no placeholders), and nothing is sent, posted or bought without his yes.
- Private life stays private: no recovery language, no therapy, legal or money matters, no real names, no family details. Run the privacy preflight before anything leaves the Mac.
- Only lines Chris marked with the nib, harvested on Sunday into `data/share-candidates/share-candidates.csv`, may be used from the diary. Unmarked diary pages never leave the vault.
- Format rules still apply: Reels first, carousels as fallback, no single images on Instagram or TikTok, TikTok first.

## How to use the share-candidates

1. Read this Lens, then `data/share-candidates/share-candidates.csv` (rows with status `share-candidate`).
2. The `brand` column says where a line belongs: `labeau`, `chris` or `both` (both brands as one voice).
3. Pick lines that fit the plan and quote them exactly. Write the hook and caption around them.
4. Record which ids you used in your own plan file. Never edit `share-candidates.csv` or this Lens; the Sunday harvest is the only writer, and blast-radius blocks AI writes to both folders.
