# The Hierophant Lens

Every agent reads this before planning anything for LaBeau or for Chris: a post, caption, hook, Substack Note, email, Reel, Gist post, research brief or social plan. Grok, Muse, the labeau-ops builder, ChatGPT, Perplexity and any Claude thread.

LaBeau and Chris's personal brand are one brand now. The diary is the work, and everything in his journal is fair game for it (his ruling, Oct 23, 2026). People's names are the one stop.

## Chris, in his own words (Oct 23, 2026)

> I'm a hierophant and my purpose is to share my perspective with a sacred bunch. I want to make sure they get how they're supposed to and how I like for them to see it as well - and that it represents me, authentically, fully, and like Rick Rubin's creative act - my diary

> I especially want it considered as we continue thinking of how to make sure my brand stands out and we package me and my diary and who I am so that my story finds its audience naturally and invites them to my experience and how we view the world.

Asked whether a reader taking one of his lines somewhere he didn't mean is a loss or the poem working, Chris answered:

> this would mean the pens working, truly

## What that means when you plan (agent guidance, not Chris's words)

1. **The diary is the art.** Present his lines as pages from a living diary, not as "content". The page, the pen and the hand belong in the frame.
2. **Show, don't preach.** A hierophant reveals what is sacred and lets people in. No advice voice, no "you should", no guru framing. Invite the reader into how he sees; never lecture them.
3. **Invite, don't control.** The aim is that readers feel invited into how he sees, not that they see it exactly as he does. A reader carrying a line somewhere new is the pen working. Leave room in the caption for that.
4. **Write to the sacred bunch, not to everyone.** Aim for the few who will feel at home. A truer post for fewer people beats a louder post for more.
5. **Whole and authentic.** Across a week, the reader should meet all of him that is public: poet, voice and rapper, the one who studies how people grow. One post can be one facet; the plan carries the whole.
6. **Process is part of the work** (Rubin, The Creative Act). Craft, attention, quiet, the moment a line arrived: these are worth showing.
7. **Let it find its audience naturally.** No tactic that costs authenticity: no bait, no fake urgency, no trend that is not his.
8. **Stop the scroll, draw in, feel at home.** Use his color research on every visual.

## Hard rules (always)

- Chris's words are never changed. Poems, diary lines and share-candidates are quoted exactly. Captions, hooks and Notes go around his words, never inside them.
- Agents write the full caption (no placeholders), and nothing is sent, posted or bought without his yes.
- No person's name ever appears in public copy. Journal lines that named someone are already swapped for the role Chris picked. Separate from the journal: no recovery language and no clinical, legal or money matters. His session transcripts are not the journal and never leave the vault. Run the privacy preflight before anything leaves the Mac.
- Journal lines come only from `data/share-candidates/cleared.csv`, quoting the `shared_line` column exactly. Never open `share-candidates.csv` beside it: it still holds lines Chris has not cleared, names included.
- Format rules still apply: Reels first, carousels as fallback, no single images on Instagram or TikTok, TikTok first.

## How to use the share-candidates

1. Read this Lens, then `data/share-candidates/cleared.csv` (every row there is cleared; use `shared_line`).
2. The `brand` column says where a line belongs: `labeau`, `chris` or `both` (both brands as one voice).
3. Pick lines that fit the plan and quote them exactly. Write the hook and caption around them.
4. Record which ids you used in your own plan file. Never edit anything in `share-candidates/` or this Lens: the journal gate and Chris's docket replies are the only writers, and blast-radius blocks AI writes to both folders.
