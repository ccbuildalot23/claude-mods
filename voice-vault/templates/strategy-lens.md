# The Strategy Lens

Every agent reads this before planning anything: LaBeau growth, a venture, the bridge itself. Grok, Hermes, Muse, the labeau-ops builder, ChatGPT, Perplexity and any Claude thread. It pairs with the Hierophant Lens (`data/hierophant-lens/hierophant-lens.md`) and does not replace it.

## Chris's rule, as he wrote it (Oct 24, 2026)

Standing rule: STRATEGY LENS (from Chris, Oct 24 2026). Add to CLAUDE.md and standing rules.
Pairs with the Hierophant Lens; does not replace it. Hierophant = whose voice and why.
Strategy Lens = how we decide.

RULE: When planning anything (LaBeau growth, a venture, the bridge itself), pick the 1-2
frameworks below that fit, name them at the top of the plan, and answer their questions
in one line each. Don't use more than 2. Don't use them for small tasks.

CHEAT SHEET (use when -> ask -> LaBeau example)
1. McKinsey 7S: a plan isn't executing -> do strategy, structure, systems, skills, values line up?
   Ex: the bot team / bridge isn't delivering.
2. GE-McKinsey 9-box: choosing where to put time -> how attractive is it, how strong are we?
   Ex: ranking TikTok, Substack, Gist, YouTube.
3. Three Horizons: balancing now vs later -> what's core today, what's emerging, what's a seed?
   Ex: H1 LaBeau, H2 Love Notes, H3 Harmoniq / Mansa / later ventures.
4. Business System: finding where value is made -> which step (create, package, distribute,
   convert) is weakest? Ex: poem -> video -> post -> subscriber.
5. Industry Cost Curve: is something worth the cost -> who does this cheapest, where are we?
   Ex: tool and AI spend vs results.
6. Consumer Decision Journey: growing an audience -> how do people find, judge, join, stay, share?
   Ex: Substack subscriber growth; loyalty loop with readers.
7. Influence Model (4 levers): building a habit -> role model, understanding, skills, reinforcement.
   Ex: my posting and writing habits.
8. SCP (Structure-Conduct-Performance): reading a platform -> how is it built, how do players act,
   who wins? Ex: Gist as an early-account opportunity.
9. Strategic Control Map: protecting the position -> how big is our reach vs how unique are we?
   Ex: owning the "poet who teaches you to read" lane.
10. Portfolio of Initiatives: picking experiments -> which are no-regrets moves, options, big bets?
   Ex: Notes test = no-regrets; Gist = option; the June 2027 reveal = big bet.

OUTPUT FORMAT: "Lens: [framework]. Answers: ... . Decision: one yes/no for Chris."
Source: Natan Mohart's McKinsey frameworks carousel (Instagram), summarised, not copied.

## How the two lenses sit together (agent guidance, not Chris's words)

- Hierophant Lens first for anything public: whose voice, and why. Strategy Lens for the decision: which move, and is it worth it.
- A plan opens with the Strategy Lens line, then the plan. A small task (one caption, one fix, one file) skips it.
- The Decision line is one question Chris can answer yes or no. It never counts as his yes until he gives it.
