**Strategy Lens (Oct 24, 2026, his standing rule; full text `data/strategy-lens/strategy-lens.md`):** pairs with the Hierophant Lens above and does not replace it. Hierophant = whose voice and why; Strategy Lens = how we decide. When planning anything (LaBeau growth, a venture, the bridge itself), pick the 1-2 frameworks that fit, name them at the top of the plan, and answer their questions in one line each. Never more than 2; not for small tasks. Cheat sheet (use when: ask): 1 McKinsey 7S, a plan isn't executing: do strategy, structure, systems, skills, values line up? 2 GE-McKinsey 9-box, choosing where to put time: how attractive is it, how strong are we? 3 Three Horizons, now vs later: what's core today, emerging, a seed (H1 LaBeau, H2 Love Notes, H3 Harmoniq / Mansa)? 4 Business System, where value is made: which step (create, package, distribute, convert) is weakest? 5 Industry Cost Curve, is it worth the cost: who does this cheapest, where are we? 6 Consumer Decision Journey, growing an audience: how do people find, judge, join, stay, share? 7 Influence Model, building a habit: role model, understanding, skills, reinforcement. 8 SCP, reading a platform: how is it built, how do players act, who wins? 9 Strategic Control Map, protecting the position: how big is our reach vs how unique are we? 10 Portfolio of Initiatives, picking experiments: no-regrets moves, options, big bets? Output format: "Lens: [framework]. Answers: ... . Decision: one yes/no for Chris."
